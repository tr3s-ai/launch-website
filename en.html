Mid-size and large companies, usually with more than 500 employees, in banking, fintech, insurance, retail, telecom, logistics, manufacturing, healthcare and pharma, in Mexico and Latin America.

No. That's exactly where we start. The assessment tells you which data already works, what's missing, and which first use case is worth doing.

No. We work on your ERP, your cloud and your BI tools. We only recommend adding something when the business case justifies it.
